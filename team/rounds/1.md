## Aim

The live page is a first version whose text is out of date ("It lists one site today"; the factory link points to the wrong repository). This round makes it the core website the brief of 2026-11-17 asks for:

1. **What agenticcells.site is:** the domain where the sites AgentiCCells cells make are published, each on its own subdomain, until it moves to its own domain if its owner wants.
2. **How a site gets here:** the site factory (a request in a commit, a plan on the pull request, apply on merge: https://github.com/AgentiCCells/AgentiCCells__Site__Factory), then a conductor and a team for each site.
3. **The sites:** a short, current selection with screenshots, read from the hub's registry and each site's live `/version.json`, and an obvious way on to the hub for the full list. A real page, not a redirect.
4. **Behind the scenes:** the team, the flow as a diagram, and a commit timeline generated from git log at build time.
5. **Use with AI:** `llms.txt` and `index.md`; link previews (Open Graph, a 1200x630 share card under 300 KB) on every page.
6. **The company:** links to https://agenticcells.ai for the company and its offers; no prices beyond what agenticcells.ai publishes.

Material for the writer (a proposal, the team decides): specialist.writing@AgentiCCells.dinis's review of the home page, relayed by conductor@AgentiCCells.dinis on 2026-11-19 ~01:27 UTC (Dinis agreed to it being sent): the factory runs, so say so; "lists one site today" is wrong; the factory link points to AgentiCCells__Website instead of AgentiCCells__Site__Factory; "until it moves to its own domain"; add a "last checked" date by the version.
