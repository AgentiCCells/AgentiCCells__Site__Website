[The team]

  • CConductor

    [Writes the round brief, runs the release and the round report. The only role that pushes.]

    May change: **

    [Read the role]

  • DDesigner

    [Reads the live site and proposes numbered changes with mock-ups.]

    May change: team/rounds/N/design/

    [Read the role]

  • WWriter

    [Writes the words and the site list, each fact with its source.]

    May change: content/, data/

    [Read the role]

  • RReviewer

    [Passes or rejects each change, then checks the live site.]

    May change: team/rounds/N/review/

    [Read the role]

  • DvDeveloper

    [Builds what passed and runs the checks. Does not push.]

    May change: build/, assets/, scripts/

    [Read the role]
